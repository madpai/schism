# Ordinary people in the same city — later

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

Local authority today owns commands/state/events. Later a server validates intent, authoring version, revision and command identity, applies transactions, and returns authoritative events/snapshot. Never accept client balance, ownership, quality or random outcomes. Local saves are offline profiles, not imported balances in a competitive world.

Candidate shared aggregates: city clock, district stock/shortages, output, vacancies, tenancies, market escrow, item custody and public notices. Private aggregates: citizen needs, room arrangement, discoveries, shift stage, choices and legal record. The server may arbitrate shared transfers while private presentation stays local. Decide online/offline reconciliation before allowing cross-player items.

Reuse original guarded stock updates, optimistic revisions, additive settlement, immutable city commitments, item escrow, bounded debt and bill-pause semantics. Preserve completed district history independent of who logs back in. Shared chat, moderation, neighbors, shops, unions, businesses and infrastructure wait until the small solo game retains players. No networking service, login dependency, public chat or MMO deployed in the Android slice.
