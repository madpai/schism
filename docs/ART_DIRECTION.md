# Recovered civic media

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

PSX analog horror industrial dystopia: angular geometry, low resolution material textures, muted concrete/oxidized green/nicotine beige/rust palette, ordered dithering, hard shadows and fluorescent darkness. Avoid modern illustration merely pixelated. The fixed camera is standing human height. Major interactable silhouettes remain unmistakable.

Original scene prompts live in mobile/art/prompts. The manifest records version, generator, SHA-256, dimensions, scene composition and normalized hotspot rectangles. Preserve original output and never falsely call nondeterministic generation reproducible: prompts reproduce intent; committed final assets reproduce builds. Object close-ups and possessions use generated raster art rather than procedural vector substitutes. The transparent 4×4 object atlas, backpack interior and three compatible laundry variants retain the original palette and geometry. Authored `mobile/data/objects.json` defines atlas cells, scene crops, compartments and state art. Native AtlasTexture regions preserve original pixels. The original full-cart frame composites into the empty-cart variant according to how many actual uniforms remain. The door-open and running frames share the same camera and architecture. Do not replace this with primitive shapes or regenerate the whole world for every button press.

A scene-only shader adds subtle UV instability, ordered dither, scanline tracking and chromatic bleed. UI text bypasses it. Reduced effects disables instability and scanlines. No high-frequency strobe. Rain and fluorescent variation animate separately. A masked shader rotates the actual raster clothes inside the glass ellipse; surrounding walls, machine frame, glass rim and knobs stay stable. Reduced effects also disables drum motion. There are no animation-driven transactions: job state remains authoritative.

Official text uses bundled Fira Sans Condensed. Human thought uses bundled italic DejaVu Serif. Font licenses are checked in; no network fonts. Original synthesized WAV ambience/effects use fixed seeds and checked-in generation source. No constant musical soundtrack or borrowed audio.
