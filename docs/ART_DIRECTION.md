Avoid modern illustration merely pixelated. The fixed camera is standing human height. Major interactable silhouettes remain unmistakable.

Original scene prompts live in mobile/art/prompts. The manifest records version, generator, SHA-256, dimensions, scene composition and normalized hotspot rectangles.
