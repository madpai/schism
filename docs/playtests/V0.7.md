# v0.7 — district life playtest and critique

October 5, 2026. The five agreed priorities are implemented: encounters from ordinary tasks, useful active/low-energy visits, funded player demand, developing shared emergencies, and distinct earned career cases. The existing local and hosted characters are preserved. No further character reset was run.

## What changed

Four finite encounters appear after actual bins/salvage/decode/shift completion. Choices affect named contacts, trust, heat, alignment, materials, and later replies. Three daily bulletins cost no energy. Quiet-route preparation lowers one crime's capture risk by ten percentage points and expires with the cycle. Overview objectives explain survival, work breaks, ID recovery, and bills; short-task energy costs are lower, with normal wages, rent, tax, permits, and residency gates retained.

The exchange accepts ten supply types and fully funded buy orders. Suppliers deliver actual stock, receive taxable payment, and can fill orders partially. Buyers reclaim only undelivered escrow when canceling. A small municipal floor has finite shared quantities and one one-unit delivery per citizen per cycle. Shops can choose more consumable stock.

Boiler/freight incidents alternate on the shared city clock. An hour of warning precedes pressure; completed repairs and diversions determine a stored outcome at a five-hour deadline. Fast direct responses consume useful supplies. Fixed targets scale from recent contributors without growing from arrivals or polling. Administration, security, shops, and underground contacts each offer one distinct short case per cycle, with free review and lawful/illicit choices behind earned access.

## Critique and corrections

The visit has more substance now: a task can reveal a person, a choice can receive a reply, and scavenged goods can satisfy actual demand. Four paid short actions left Esren with 39 energy, preserving room for employment. Lawful choices offer trust and useful output; illegal alternatives offer more immediate money with heat and shared harm. The tiny paid rewards and daily caps keep this route from replacing wages.

The mobile review found that the area gallery pushed the emergency controls far down the city page. The live incident now leads, and the gallery is expandable. The compact incident also moved above overview vitals. A second inconsistency was the legacy bag calling itself empty despite held salvage; it now includes materials and crafted supplies, with appropriate consumable controls. Inventory counts and filters also include these items instead of showing an empty materials category above a separate salvage ledger. Original scenes give the encounters and case desks a consistent place in the city without adding incompatible portrait layers.

The remaining weaknesses are content variety and accumulated page length. Four finite neighborhood stories will be exhausted; each institution currently repeats one case; the emergency alternates only two types. Outcomes affect the final hour of the cycle, and longer consequences need deliberate recovery rules. The authored municipal/case buyers make quiet-city participation possible but are not evidence of a mature player economy. Multi-day net income, fill rates, exhaustion, and housing progression still need actual player observation. Current offline settlement uses current weather/heating, not a reconstruction of every missed incident.

This is a stronger short visit with harsh resource constraints, not proof of long-term MMORPG retention. [NEXT_STEPS](../NEXT_STEPS.md) records concrete follow-on work: contacts reacting to history, more case/emergency variety, real demand measurements, multi-day recovery, and shorter mobile presentation. Native WebMCP remains unverified; chat uses polling and has no private messages or push notifications.
