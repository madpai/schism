# The object is the opportunity

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

Found objects have a rightful owner, origin, serial, condition, legal status and custody history. Put in pocket marks an unlawful ownership transfer and unreported money. Lost Property retains the actual object under institutional custody; leave retains it in its origin. Resolved opportunities cannot be rerolled by reloading.

Risk is legible: a logged security jacket has an inventory receipt; a camera watches the desk; a freight seal appears on the manifest. Risk is recorded when the object is kept; a deterministic random stream advances once at settlement. Outcome events state the evidence, confiscation and fine. No unexplained arbitrary arrest. Repeated caught theft leads to three compulsory work orders. A record flag survives release.

Camp pauses outside housing billing, stores belongings with custody history and pauses employment. Playable scrap sorting, laundry/cleaning stations, miserable meal and bunk, three work orders, no wages. Release restores held possessions and outside life. Further prisoner trading, smuggling, escape and corruption are roadmap systems, not opening requirements.

Crime is petty in this build. Escalation can add access logs, serial tracing, black market demand, fines/debt and detention intake without a generic crime menu.
