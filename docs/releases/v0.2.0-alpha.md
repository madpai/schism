An ordinary life in District IX. A portrait, offline Android point-and-click development preview.

This update makes room storage real: belongings can stay in the locker while you work; food can go into an acquired refrigerator and age more slowly while its power works. Food in your bag no longer benefits just because you own a fridge. The laundry cart now contains four large, tappable garments rather than a list of uniform rows.

Install `schism-0.2.0.apk` on Android 7.0 or newer (arm64 or x86_64). To update 0.1.0, install over the existing app; do not uninstall. The package and signing certificate are unchanged. Schema migration preserves your citizen, wallet, needs, job history, possessions and existing fridge allowance.

Verified: 405 core rule checks, 75 storage checks, 109 native UI checks, ordinary five-shift survival/kettle balance, asset provenance, in-place Android upgrade, storage recovery after process death, and the full touch playthrough at three portrait sizes. Device coverage is an Android 14 ANGLE emulator; physical-phone audio, ergonomics and broader GPU coverage still need review.

The APK is signed with the project's development debug key. This is a sideload preview, not a production Play Store release. No account, internet permission or paid energy is required. `manifest.json` records the source commit, package, signing-certificate fingerprint and APK hash. `SHA256SUMS` verifies both files.
