# SCHISM 0.6.0 — Hands-on sanitation and freight

Sanitation and freight now require individual physical actions with animated feedback and saved progress.

- Mop three muddy floor bands and wipe three desk patches. Tie the bin bag, lift it out and fit a fresh liner.
- Turn each freight parcel to read its label, lift it onto the trolley, stamp it and drag it into its outgoing lane.
- Every gesture has a large tap alternative. Reduced effects keeps each work step playable without motion.
- Completed actions survive interruption. Closing a close-up cancels an unfinished animation; duplicate or skipped steps cannot complete work.
- Desk and damaged-parcel discoveries retain their custody choices. Wages, taxes, quality rules and four-hour shifts are preserved.
