> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

Municipal Laundry Attendant: 7 CR gross, four uniforms per authored batch, category/stain/pocket metadata. Factory/oil uses hot wash; medical/blood sanitizes; mixed general laundry uses standard. Supply/tools belong to the workplace. Found 3 CR are never displayed as a moral button. Use “Lost Property tray”, “Put in pocket” or “Leave it”. First mystery is a tiny damaged training tape on a later shift.

Civic Sanitation Worker: 8 CR gross. Mop/wipe dirty surfaces, replace trash bags, inspect forgotten desk objects. Freight Sorter: 9 CR gross. Inspect labels/manifest and route crates into named lanes; damaged cargo creates discrepancies. Mistakes lower quality and trust; wages have a floor and an itemized receipt.

Shift IDs, resolved pockets, selected treatment, machine stage, settlement receipt and output events persist. Settlement is idempotent. A future authority verifies commands and issues output once; UI never computes pay.
