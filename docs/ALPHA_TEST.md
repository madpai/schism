# SCHISM invited alpha

Use v0.9 with 10–20 named testers for one or two real weeks. Keep the current private audience until tester identities are supplied. A domain is optional for this test; the existing hosted address is sufficient. Actual invitations and DNS setup still need the owner's tester list and chosen hostname.

## First visit

Give players only the link and this premise: arrive in a cold city with no credits; find a way to survive and someone worth returning for. Observe before explaining the controls. Let them choose how to spend the visit.

Record whether they find the stair story, understand energy and completion times, recover the lamp supplies, finish a meaningful choice, and start paid work within their first ten minutes. A light on the stair can use entirely owned supplies, so an empty association does not stop it. Its next-cycle message supplies a reason to return. Do not promise that every player will choose or complete the same route.

## A real week

Invite a mix of once-daily visitors, twice-daily visitors, and people who miss a day or weekend. Do not require visits on six-hour boundaries. Ask players to note visit duration, confusing screens, food/warmth spending, recovery after absence, goals achieved, and whether they wanted to return. Pair some players in an association to test requests, actual donated supplies, shared repairs, and messages. Observe whether help happens without the operator supplying resources.

Use **Send game feedback** on any registered screen. Community messages offer private reports. The configured operator reads both in the expandable moderation panel, can hide reported messages, and can pause their author's posting for 24 hours. These controls do not prevent game play. Do not include personal contact details in game messages or feedback.

## Broad-launch gate

Target these concrete outcomes before opening public access:

- At least 8 of 10 observed first-time players can explain their next step and finish a meaningful story or resource decision within ten minutes without coaching.
- All tested missed-day and missed-weekend accounts can return to useful play; no endless basic-housing debt or duplicated timer rewards.
- Daily players perceive at least one reachable progression goal; record private-room deposit and recurring-rent affordability separately.
- At least three associations exchange real supplies and finish requested work; measure fulfilled requests and wait times rather than assuming demand.
- Zero unresolved save-loss, privacy, ownership, or duplicate-reward bugs. Investigate any reproducible blocking mobile issue before launch.
- A protected city backup is downloaded, restored to an isolated database, and checked before each significant schema deployment. Verify the hosted recovery procedure with the operator before relying on it.
- Hosted monitoring has an explicit owner: inspect health and errors during the test, review reports daily, and record API latency/errors under real internet conditions.

These are acceptance targets, not completed human results. Automated checks and a local load test do not establish enjoyment, actual phone usability, hosted D1 capacity, or a week of retention. Begin with a review after the first ten sessions; revise the build before enlarging the group if the evidence calls for it.
