# Living through objects

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

Tap the door, stairwell and named building entrances to travel. Tap a bed to rest; the sink to drink/wash; the locker to inspect belongings. Status and inventory are temporary overlays. A small location caption, civic balance and optional hotspot hints are the persistent interface.

Labor allocation: take ticket C-184, offer identity at window 3, inspect three physical vacancy papers. Laundry pays 7 CR gross, sanitation 8, freight 9. An application grants employment, not wages. Switching jobs preserves each record.

Laundry is a staged machine activity: inspect each garment and pocket, choose a physical sorting bin, open hatch, load the sorted bundle, dose detergent, choose a cycle, shut hatch, start, unload, dry, fold, dispatch, collect stamped wage slip. Tap alternatives cover all dragging. Machine animation is short; no real thirty-minute wait. Saving halfway preserves the exact pocket decisions and machine stage. Different categories/stains require standard/hot/sanitize treatment; labels explain the rule. Incorrect preparation creates visible quality damage and warnings, rather than resetting work.

Cleaning: use supplies, clean floor patches, replace bin bags, inspect a desk and return/keep its object, collect wage slip. Freight: inspect real labels, compare manifest, route individual crates to matching outgoing lanes, handle damaged seals, inspect/keep/return contents, settle quality and pay. These use the same shift/settlement/ownership framework but smaller activities than laundry.

After work, buy actual wrapped food or bottled water at the stall, carry it home or consume from the bag. A free public tap, emergency meal when broke, and bed always give a recovery route. Internal thoughts appear briefly without confirmation. Twelve shifts reveal an internal vacancy on the bureau noticeboard.

Wages are paid in full. Civic tax is assessed at 12%, with fractional carry, and paid manually at the administrative counter in the labor bureau. Every three played days the current assessments become an invoice, followed by one day of grace. The payment slip shows the balance and deadline; full and partial payments are available. Overdue accounts receive a street inspection warning: go directly to the bureau to pay, or face compulsory work on another journey. A service-gate inspection can also enforce the debt. Correction orders produce city supplies and work off one credit each, with a minimum of three orders for tax detention. Outside billing pauses during detention; closing the game never advances deadlines.

The service corridor connects normally to the street. Examine the failed cabinet and choose to repair it whenever you want. The repair restores lighting, earns a security favor and makes the relay recording available immediately. The gate officer recognizes that favor during ordinary paper inspections. Neighbors can need help carrying a basket or sharing food; a coworker can offer an extra station to clear. Choices and cooldowns are saved. Encounters do not depend on returning at a prescribed hour.

New residents choose one of five civilian backgrounds on the residency paper. Starts have equal credits and needs; paper mementos and contextual knowledge differ. Existing residents retain a neutral history and their belongings. All occupations and major opportunities remain open.
