# SCHISM — a life in District IX

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

You are an ordinary poor citizen. Arrive with 4 CR, an identity paper and Block C Room 17. Your first ambition is a job. The emotional anchor is your room: a kettle should feel like a promotion.

The design pillars are physical continuity, readable touch interaction, menial work with small decisions, persistent objects, poverty without absence punishment, and ambiguous institutional horror. The city speaks in condensed official type; the citizen thinks in rough, quiet subtitles. No chosen one, opening monster, gacha, energy purchases or endless task list.

The first session follows arrival → room → hallway → street → ticket → clerk → job → laundry → wage slip → food stall → room → bed. Lost property creates temptation. A damaged Floor 4 training tape creates a question, not a quest.

Original Canon/Wound and Ninth Stratum lore is historical research. District IX and civic identity survive. Overt neural supernatural descriptions move behind damaged recordings. Nobody explains the entire city on arrival.

Success: “I worked laundry, ate, kept money from a jacket, bought soap, and now I’m saving for a kettle.” Human retention and actual phone comfort remain release gates.
