# The emotional anchor

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

Block C Room 17 is free municipal housing with a thin mattress and unreliable aesthetic infrastructure, but an always-available water/recovery route. The tenancy record tracks address, tier, utility condition, next bill, arrears, deposit and paid totals. A registry paper in the room explains the current arrangement.

Room possessions are real owned instances. Blanket 9 CR improves rest; mug 3; kettle 18 enables tea; radio 24 gives damaged media playback; shelf 8; refrigerator 60 improves food storage; private room deposit 25 and rent 2 per played day, better apartment deposit 80 and rent 4. Room art overlays change with acquisitions. The first kettle is an achievable savings goal.

Rent is assessed only when action-time crosses a played-day deadline. Four unpaid bills cap arrears and return housing to free municipal shelter, preserving ownership and debt records. A resident may return to free housing at any time off duty. Camp advances the billing deadline by exactly the time in custody. No offline rent avalanche or duplicate bill on resume.

The locker and installed refrigerator have actual contents. Stored items keep their serial, rightful owner, legality and custody history. The bag shows only carried items. Leaving home prevents consuming or retrieving stored food remotely. Household storage stays home during detention; carried belongings are held. Storage currently follows the resident when changing tenancy; packing/moving between separate addresses is future content. Refrigerator preservation depends on stored placement and working utilities, rather than a household-wide food bonus.
