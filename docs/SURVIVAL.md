# Time and necessities

> DO NOT TURN SCHISM BACK INTO A MENU-HEAVY DASHBOARD GAME. The player interacts with places and objects.

All need values mean reserve: 100 is fed/hydrated/rested/clean/healthy. Status says “Hunger / fed” to avoid inverted gauges. Start mildly pressured: food 52, water 46, energy 58, hygiene 48, health 91. Action-time decay: per in-game hour food −4, water −6, idle fatigue −1, hygiene −1. Work also spends energy. Below 15 food or water damages health; health has a survivable floor. No needs tick while reading, waiting for animations, paused or absent.

Travel costs five in-game minutes. A shift costs four in-game hours spread across meaningful stages. Sleep advances eight in-game hours at lower food/water decay, restores energy according to bed quality and heals slightly when fed. Time begins Day 1 at 06:00. No shared six-hour day. Offline time is frozen. Future offline city notices may accumulate, but cannot silently kill or evict a citizen.

Bread 3 CR (+42 food); ration paste 2 (+30); hot stew 5 (+65 food, +3 health); bottled water 1 (+50 water); soap 1 (three washes); tea 2. Bread lasts three simulation days, stew one. Food must be placed in the powered refrigerator to earn up to three extra played days of preservation. Food in the bag or locker ages normally. Taking food out keeps its earned cooling but adds no more; spoiled food cannot be revived by moving it into the fridge. Consumption rejects spoiled items. Storage never ticks on wall-clock absence. Public and room tap water is free if utilities function. Sink washing without soap helps a little, with soap substantially. Hygiene is a gradual consequence, not a repeated minute-by-minute chore.

Wages withhold floor(gross × 12% + carried fraction); remainder persists so splitting pay cannot avoid taxes. Laundry’s first 7 CR settlement pays 7; cleaning’s first 8 pays 8; later carried fractions produce 1 CR withholding. Wage slip shows gross, withholding and net. No separate tax-debt UI in the opening.

A broke resident may request one free meal per simulation day; water and sleep remain free. No monetized recovery.
