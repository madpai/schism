# SCHISM v0.10: repair, signals and custody

The free communal workbench connects salvaging to usable equipment and technical work. No character reset or new sign-in is required. New fields receive backward-compatible defaults in the citizen's authoritative D1 record.

## Practical skills

Electronics, mechanics, tailoring and signal analysis start at level 0. Levels 1, 2 and 3 require 6, 18 and 40 XP. A matching skill level adds 1 CR to job pay and saves 1 energy. The original career XP, trust, permit and residency gates remain. Four new level-1 technical jobs accept practical training without demanding a career rank.

Practice arrives only at completion, with a maximum 12 XP per skill per six-hour cycle. Communal lessons take 30 seconds and 3 energy, give 2 XP, and are available once per skill per cycle. Finished relevant repairs and jobs also train skills. Dismantling gives parts and no XP. Neri, Esra, Rook and Voss are associated with tailoring, electronics, mechanics and signals; completed lessons deepen the corresponding contact record.

A beginner can find a PDA, diagnose it, scavenge wire/circuit parts, replace its battery using supplied bench tools, and complete an electronics lesson to qualify for handheld repair work. Mechanics can be learned through a hand generator repair; tailoring through dressings and weather linings; signal analysis through legal diagnostics, decoding and lessons.

## Unique devices and useful functions

Each PDA, field receiver or hand-generator lamp has a server-created identity, condition, fault, diagnosis and repair quality. At most eight are carried. Two device finds per cycle and the ordinary ten-task allowance bound salvaging. The first two finds have beginner battery faults; later contacts and encrypted faults require appropriate level-1 skills. Diagnosis, ownership and repair condition become available at their deadlines, never before.

Basic repairs spend one wire and one circuit; a rebuilt power cell can substitute. Contact repairs spend two wire and a circuit. Encrypted recovery needs two traces and a circuit. Quality is 1 plus the repairing skill level. Repaired PDAs improve legal trace recovery, receivers reduce illicit terminal detection by five percentage points, and lamps reduce idle warmth loss by 0.2 per city hour. One handheld can be equipped.

A broken device sells to the municipal refurbisher for 2 CR; a repaired device for 10–16 CR. Demand is six devices across the city and one sale per citizen per cycle. Proceeds are reported income. Citizen listings hold the actual device in escrow, preserve its fault/quality, and atomically transfer ownership and payment. Listing or dismantling removes its equipped effect. Cancellation restores the same device.

## Crafting, comfort and fictional restricted goods

Rebuilt cells and weather linings join the existing heat packs, dressings, relay fragments and neural patches. Linings provide 18 warmth and reduce recorded clothing wear by 20. Bench recipes reserve inputs and energy, then deliver the product and practice when finished.

Stress is visible in the comfort workbench. At 70 stress, work costs two extra energy. Sleep, tea and fireside conversation lower it. Tea gives 10 less stress and four warmth. Cigarettes give 12 less stress and cost 0.5 health. Tea/cigarette comfort can be used once per city hour. Both can be bought; fictional reclaimed leaf also enables rolling a pack. Neither is required for progression.

Nightglass and Veil are invented contraband made from fictional clinic gel and electronic salvage, with no real drug chemistry. Nightglass gives 18 energy, then after 30 real minutes removes 12 energy, adds 15 stress and costs two health. Veil removes 30 stress and adds eight coherence, then removes eight energy and twelve coherence at its deadline. Only one street-drug effect can be active. Crashes persist and resolve once even offline.

Restricted gel itself, Nightglass and Veil are visibly marked contraband in crafting, inventory, requests and trade. Units may be stored in the municipal locker, taken back out, or surrendered without a fine. Locker transfers conserve stock. Public association shelves do not accept contraband.

## Signals and detection

Public diagnostics and encrypted echo recovery are legal. Manifest alteration and a buried contract are fictional intrusions into the game's civic terminals. Trained signal skill and repaired equipment unlock these contracts and change detection risk. The server chooses and persists the outcome when the task starts. One acceptance per contract per cycle prevents repeated payouts. Costs, timers and risk are shown before an illicit choice.

A clinic discard supplies one fictional gel and civic leaf. A risky crate theft can recover two gels and a leaf; detection counts as an offense. Scans happen on an explicit 30-second checkpoint crossing, once per cycle. Refreshing never rolls another inspection. Risk includes heat, current inspection conditions and city security.

A detected small first possession confiscates carried contraband, fines up to five available CR and leaves an arrest hold. Bulk possession of three or more units, or a second detected offense, relocates the citizen to compulsory labor. Stored units remain in the locker. Registry clearance remains a distinct step after a sentence or fine.

## Municipal labor camp

Every sentence requires exactly three completed production orders and pays zero CR. Basic ration packing is open without skills; electronics and mechanics unlock relay/loom assignments. Skills shorten assignments from 60 seconds toward 30 and lower effort from seven toward three energy. Inputs and tools are supplied. Each assignment records a real municipal shipment and a timed city output contribution. Completed output replenishes shared food stock subject to the existing capacity; relay work also adds district relief.

A free fifteen-second meal/rest restores 25 energy, 24 fullness, 15 warmth and four health, while lowering stress by eight. It has no energy, credit or material requirement and remains available between assignments. Camp time does not advance the outside world or finish orders early. The quota cannot grow offline or grant duplicate release credit. Outside private rent and tax deadlines pause from relocation until release and resume by exactly that interval; existing debts and housing are retained.

Ivo can be helped, and a false count refused, for finite trust/stress choices without free release credit. Signal level 2 opens one escape attempt: 35% success, eight energy and 60 seconds; failure adds ten heat without extending the work sentence, success adds twenty heat and resumes bills. The ordinary three-order release route remains available. Release leaves an arrest record for clearance and supplies enough basic vitals to return to the city.

## Evidence and current limits

Automated rules and built-Worker checks cover deferred rewards, zero-credit access, item/payment races, finite demand, drug deadlines, confiscation, storage, fixed custody, shared output and backup tables. Browser evidence distinguishes ordinary timed play on the persistent city from isolated fixtures used to inspect camp and higher-skill states. This is an expanded private alpha; human multi-day balance and production D1 latency still need observation before opening a public audience.
